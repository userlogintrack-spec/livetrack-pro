Pricing FAQ

Is there really a free plan?

Yes — Free is genuinely free forever, no credit card. It caps at 100 visitors/month and 1 agent. Upgrade only when you outgrow that.

Can I switch plans later?

Anytime. Upgrades take effect immediately, downgrades take effect at the end of your current billing cycle. No prorated charges to worry about.

What's your refund policy?

14-day money-back guarantee on any paid plan. Email us within 14 days and we'll refund — no questions asked. See Refund Policy.

Do you offer annual billing?

Yes. Switch the toggle above to annual and save 20% on Pro and Enterprise — billed once a year.

Are there setup or hidden fees?

None. The price you see is the price you pay. No per-seat surcharges, no message overages, no "AI add-on" tax.

How does the AI bot cost work?

The Enterprise plan ships with an AI bot, but you bring your own Anthropic API key — so you pay Anthropic directly for your usage, with no markup from us.
